# TechNova Solutions - Code of Conduct

Document ID: DOC005

## Purpose

The Code of Conduct describes the professional standards expected from employees at TechNova Solutions.

## Professional Behavior

Employees are expected to communicate respectfully with colleagues, managers, customers, and other people they interact with through their work.

Employees should act honestly and responsibly when performing their duties.

## Respectful Workplace

TechNova Solutions aims to maintain a workplace where employees can work without harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or inappropriate behavior.

Employees should treat others fairly and respect different opinions and working styles.

## Communication

Employees should use professional language in emails, meetings, messaging platforms, and other workplace communications.

Disagreements should be handled constructively and escalated through the appropriate channels when necessary.

## Confidential Information

Employees may have access to company, customer, or project information as part of their responsibilities.

Such information should only be accessed, used, or shared for legitimate business purposes and according to applicable company procedures.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees should disclose situations that could create a conflict between their personal interests and their responsibilities to the company.

Potential conflicts should be discussed with the appropriate manager or designated company representative.

## Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, systems, accounts, and other resources should be used responsibly and primarily for authorized business activities.

Employees should protect company resources from unauthorized access or misuse.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees who become aware of serious workplace concerns should report them through the appropriate company channel.

Reports should be handled responsibly and information should only be shared with people who need to know.

## Responsibilities

Every employee is responsible for following the Code of Conduct.

Managers are responsible for promoting professional behavior and addressing concerns appropriately.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update the Code of Conduct when business requirements or workplace practices change.
