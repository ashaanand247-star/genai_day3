# TechNova Solutions - Work From Home Policy

Document ID: DOC002

## Purpose

The Work From Home Policy provides guidelines for employees who are approved to work remotely.

## Eligibility

Employees may work from home when their role, team responsibilities, and business requirements allow remote work. Approval may depend on operational needs and the employee's work responsibilities.

## Work Schedule

Employees working remotely are expected to follow their assigned working hours. They should remain available through approved communication channels during working hours.

## Availability

Employees should respond to work-related messages and attend scheduled meetings while working remotely. If an employee becomes temporarily unavailable, they should inform their manager or team.

## Workspace

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable workspace that allows them to perform their duties effectively. Company equipment should be used responsibly and protected from unauthorized access.

## Information Security

Employees must follow company security requirements while working remotely. Confidential company information should not be shared with unauthorized individuals.

Employees should use approved systems and networks for accessing company resources.

## Communication

Teams should use approved communication and collaboration tools for work-related discussions. Important decisions and updates should be recorded in the appropriate team channels or systems.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review this policy periodically and make changes based on business requirements, technology, or operational needs.
