# TechNova Solutions - Employee Leave Policy

Document ID: DOC001

## Purpose

The Employee Leave Policy explains the types of leave available to employees at TechNova Solutions and the basic process for requesting leave.

## Annual Leave

Employees can request annual leave for personal requirements, planned vacations, or other approved purposes. Employees should submit their leave request in advance whenever possible.

## Sick Leave

Employees may use sick leave when they are unable to work because of illness. For an extended absence, the employee may be asked to provide appropriate supporting documentation according to company procedures.

## Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected personal or family situation requires immediate attention. Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Leave Request Process

Employees should submit leave requests through the approved internal leave system. The request should include the requested dates and the type of leave.

Managers review requests based on team requirements and planned workloads. Employees should wait for approval before treating planned leave as confirmed.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for submitting accurate leave requests and keeping their manager informed about unexpected absences.

Managers are responsible for reviewing leave requests fairly and communicating approval or rejection to the employee.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when business requirements or internal procedures change.
