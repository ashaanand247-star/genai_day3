# TechNova Solutions - Travel Policy

Document ID: DOC008

## Purpose

The Travel Policy provides guidelines for employees who travel for approved business purposes.

## Travel Approval

Employees should obtain the required approval before making business travel arrangements.

The travel request should include the purpose of the trip, expected dates, destination, and other relevant details.

## Transportation

Employees should use reasonable and cost-effective transportation options for business travel.

The appropriate transportation method may depend on the distance, location, business requirement, and travel schedule.

## Accommodation

Employees should use approved accommodation options where available.

Accommodation should be selected based on reasonable cost, location, safety, and business requirements.

## Travel Expenses

Reasonable business travel expenses may be reimbursed when they are supported by appropriate documentation and comply with company guidelines.

Employees should retain receipts and other required records.

## Meals

Employees may claim eligible meal expenses incurred during approved business travel according to the applicable expense guidelines.

Personal expenses that are not related to the business trip should not be submitted for reimbursement.

## Changes and Cancellations

Employees should inform the appropriate manager or travel coordinator if approved travel plans need to be changed or cancelled.

Employees should make reasonable efforts to avoid unnecessary cancellation costs.

## International Travel

Employees traveling internationally are responsible for ensuring that they have the required travel documents and approvals before departure.

Additional requirements may apply depending on the destination.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for following travel approval procedures and submitting accurate expense claims.

Managers are responsible for reviewing business travel requests and ensuring that travel is appropriate for the business requirement.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy based on business requirements, travel conditions, or changes to internal procedures.
