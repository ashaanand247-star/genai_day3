# TechNova Solutions - Resignation and Exit Process

Document ID: DOC026

## Purpose

The Resignation and Exit Process describes the general steps employees should follow when leaving TechNova Solutions.

## Resignation

Employees who decide to leave the company should submit their resignation through the approved process.

The resignation should include the required information and should follow the applicable notice requirements.

## Notice Period

Employees are expected to follow the notice period specified in their employment terms or applicable company procedures.

Managers and employees should coordinate responsibilities during the notice period.

## Knowledge Transfer

Employees may be required to document ongoing work, important tasks, project information, and other relevant knowledge before their final working day.

Knowledge transfer should be completed in coordination with the manager or designated team members.

## Company Assets

Employees must return company-owned equipment, access cards, documents, and other assigned assets according to the exit procedure.

The relevant team may verify the return of company assets.

## System Access

Access to company systems may be reviewed or disabled as part of the exit process.

Employees should not attempt to access company systems after their authorized access has ended.

## Final Documentation

Employees may need to complete required exit forms or other documentation before their final working day.

The appropriate internal teams may provide instructions about the required steps.

## Final Settlement

Any applicable final salary, reimbursements, or other payments will be processed according to company procedures and applicable requirements.

## Exit Discussion

The company may conduct an exit discussion to collect feedback about the employee's experience and identify areas for improvement.

Participation should follow the applicable company process.

## Confidentiality

Employees are expected to continue protecting confidential company and customer information after leaving the company where applicable.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for completing required exit activities and returning company property.

Managers are responsible for coordinating knowledge transfer and supporting an orderly transition.

## Process Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update the exit process when organizational or operational requirements change.
