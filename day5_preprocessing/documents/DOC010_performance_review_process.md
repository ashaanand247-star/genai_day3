# TechNova Solutions - Performance Review Process

Document ID: DOC010

## Purpose

The Performance Review Process provides a structured way to discuss employee performance, achievements, development areas, and future goals.

## Review Cycle

TechNova Solutions may conduct performance reviews at scheduled intervals. Employees and managers should follow the review schedule communicated by the company.

## Goal Setting

At the beginning of a review period, employees and managers may agree on goals related to the employee's responsibilities and team objectives.

Goals should be clear enough to allow progress to be discussed during the review.

## Performance Discussion

The manager and employee discuss completed work, achievements, challenges, collaboration, and progress toward agreed goals.

The discussion should provide an opportunity for the employee to ask questions and share feedback.

## Feedback

Managers should provide constructive feedback based on the employee's work and responsibilities.

Employees are encouraged to discuss areas where they need additional guidance or resources.

## Development Plan

When appropriate, the manager and employee may create a development plan that identifies skills, training, responsibilities, or experiences that can support future growth.

## Review Outcome

The review may include an overall assessment of performance and progress.

The outcome should be communicated clearly to the employee and recorded through the approved company process.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for preparing for performance discussions and participating openly in the review process.

Managers are responsible for providing fair, constructive, and role-relevant feedback.

## Confidentiality

Performance review information should be handled appropriately and shared only with authorized individuals.

## Process Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update the performance review process based on organizational requirements and feedback.
