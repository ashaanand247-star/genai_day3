# TechNova Solutions - Laptop Usage Policy

Document ID: DOC014

## Purpose

The Laptop Usage Policy provides guidelines for employees who use company-provided laptops for their work.

## Company Equipment

Company laptops are provided to support approved business activities. Employees should use the assigned equipment responsibly and protect it from damage, loss, or unauthorized use.

## Security

Employees should use the security controls provided on company laptops, including approved authentication methods and security software.

Employees should lock their laptop when leaving it unattended.

## Software Installation

Employees should install software only when it is approved or required for their work.

Unapproved software may introduce security, compatibility, or licensing risks.

## Password Protection

Employees should protect their account credentials and should not share passwords with other people.

If an employee suspects that their credentials have been compromised, they should report the issue through the appropriate IT or security channel.

## Remote Use

Employees working remotely should keep company laptops in a secure location and avoid allowing unauthorized people to use the device.

Company information should not be exposed to unauthorized individuals.

## Physical Protection

Employees should take reasonable precautions when carrying company laptops outside the office.

The laptop should not be left unattended in locations where it could be easily lost or stolen.

## Technical Problems

Employees should report hardware or software problems through the approved IT support process.

Employees should not attempt unauthorized hardware modifications or repairs.

## Loss or Theft

If a company laptop is lost or stolen, the employee should report the incident to the appropriate IT or security team as soon as possible.

## Return of Equipment

Employees must return company equipment when requested or when their employment or assignment ends, according to the applicable exit procedure.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of assigned equipment and following security requirements.

The IT team is responsible for maintaining applicable technical controls and providing support for company equipment.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when technology, security requirements, or equipment procedures change.
