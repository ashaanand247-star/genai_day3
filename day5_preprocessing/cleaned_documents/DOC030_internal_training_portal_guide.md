# TechNova Solutions - Internal Training Portal Guide

Document ID: DOC030

## Purpose

The Internal Training Portal Guide explains how employees can access and use the company's training resources.

## Portal Access

Employees can access the training portal using their approved company account.

Employees should not share their portal credentials with another person.

## Training Dashboard

The training dashboard displays assigned courses, available learning resources, and the progress of completed training activities.

Employees should review the dashboard regularly to identify pending training requirements.

## Assigned Training

Some training courses may be mandatory based on an employee's role, team, or company requirements.

Employees should complete assigned training within the specified completion period.

## Course Enrollment

Employees may enroll in available courses when enrollment is open.

Some courses may require manager approval or may be limited to specific roles or teams.

## Training Materials

The portal may provide videos, documents, presentations, assessments, and other learning materials.

Employees should use the materials provided through approved company systems.

## Assessments

Some courses may include quizzes, tests, or other assessments.

Employees should complete assessments according to the instructions provided with the course.

## Completion Records

Completed training activities may be recorded automatically in the training portal.

Employees should verify that required training shows the correct completion status.

If a completed course is not recorded correctly, the employee should contact the appropriate training support team.

## Training Deadlines

Employees are responsible for completing mandatory training before the assigned deadline.

Managers may follow up when required training remains incomplete.

## Technical Issues

Problems accessing the training portal or completing a course should be reported through the approved IT support process.

Employees should provide relevant information about the issue when submitting a support request.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for completing assigned training and maintaining accurate training records.

Managers are responsible for communicating role-specific training requirements and supporting employee development.

## Guide Review

TechNova Solutions may update the training portal and this guide when training systems, procedures, or learning requirements change.
