# TechNova Solutions - IT Support Guidelines

Document ID: DOC013

## Purpose

The IT Support Guidelines explain how employees should request technical assistance and report common technology-related issues.

## Requesting Support

Employees should use the approved IT support system to report technical problems.

The request should include a clear description of the problem, the affected system or device, and any relevant error message.

## Priority

IT support requests may be assigned different priorities based on their business impact and urgency.

Issues affecting critical business operations may receive higher priority than individual issues with available workarounds.

## Hardware Issues

Employees should report problems with company laptops, monitors, keyboards, network equipment, or other approved hardware through the IT support process.

Employees should not attempt repairs that could damage company equipment unless specifically authorized.

## Software Issues

Employees should report problems with approved software applications through the IT support system.

Where possible, employees should provide the application name, version, description of the problem, and steps that caused the issue.

## Account Access

Requests involving account access or permissions should identify the system and the business reason for the requested access.

Access changes may require approval from the employee's manager or an authorized system owner.

## Security Incidents

Suspected security incidents should be reported through the designated security or IT support channel as soon as possible.

Employees should not attempt to investigate or resolve suspected security incidents beyond their authorized responsibilities.

## Communication

Employees should respond to reasonable requests for additional information from the IT support team.

If an issue is resolved, the employee should confirm the resolution when requested.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for providing accurate information when submitting support requests and following IT instructions.

The IT support team is responsible for handling requests according to applicable support procedures.

## Guidelines Review

TechNova Solutions may update these guidelines when IT systems, support procedures, or security requirements change.
