# TechNova Solutions - Internet Usage Policy

Document ID: DOC016

## Purpose

The Internet Usage Policy provides guidelines for using internet services and network resources provided by TechNova Solutions.

## Business Use

Company internet access should primarily be used for legitimate business activities related to an employee's responsibilities.

Employees should use network resources responsibly and avoid activities that could negatively affect business operations.

## Personal Use

Limited personal internet use may be permitted when it does not interfere with work responsibilities, violate company policies, or create security risks.

Employees should avoid excessive personal use during working hours.

## Restricted Activities

Employees must not use company networks to access, distribute, or store content that violates applicable laws or company policies.

Activities that could introduce security risks or interfere with company systems are not permitted.

## Downloads

Employees should download files and software only from trusted and approved sources.

Unnecessary downloads can consume network resources and may introduce security risks.

## Security

Employees should be cautious when accessing websites, links, and online services.

Suspicious websites, downloads, or security warnings should be reported through the appropriate IT or security channel.

## Remote Access

Employees accessing company resources remotely should use approved access methods and follow applicable security requirements.

Employees should avoid accessing sensitive company resources through unauthorized devices or networks.

## Monitoring

Company network and internet activity may be monitored according to applicable company policies and requirements.

Monitoring is intended to support security, compliance, and reliable operation of company systems.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for using internet resources appropriately and following company security requirements.

The IT team is responsible for maintaining network services and applying appropriate technical controls.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when technology, security requirements, or business practices change.
