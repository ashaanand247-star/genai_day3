# TechNova Solutions - Training and Development

Document ID: DOC012

## Purpose

The Training and Development Policy describes how employees can participate in learning activities that support their current responsibilities and future professional development.

## Training Needs

Training needs may be identified through onboarding, performance discussions, changes in technology, new responsibilities, or team requirements.

Managers and employees should discuss relevant learning opportunities when a development need is identified.

## Internal Training

TechNova Solutions may provide internal training sessions, workshops, technical demonstrations, and learning materials.

Employees should attend mandatory training assigned to their role within the required period.

## External Training

Employees may request approval to participate in external courses, conferences, or other professional learning activities when the activity is relevant to their responsibilities.

Approval may depend on business requirements and available resources.

## Technical Development

Employees working in technical roles are encouraged to maintain their knowledge of relevant tools, technologies, and development practices.

Teams may recommend learning resources based on project requirements.

## Training Records

Completion of mandatory training may be recorded in the approved training system.

Employees should ensure that required training activities are completed and recorded correctly.

## Development Goals

Employees may include learning and development goals as part of their performance or career discussions.

Development goals should be realistic and relevant to the employee's role or career direction.

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers should help employees identify relevant development opportunities and provide reasonable support for approved learning activities.

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for actively participating in assigned training and applying relevant learning to their work.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review this policy periodically to ensure that training programs continue to meet organizational and employee development needs.
