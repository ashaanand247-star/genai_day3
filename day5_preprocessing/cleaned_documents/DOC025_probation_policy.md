# TechNova Solutions - Probation Policy

Document ID: DOC025

## Purpose

The Probation Policy explains the general expectations and review process for employees during their probation period.

## Probation Period

New employees may be placed on a probation period according to the terms communicated at the time of joining.

The probation period provides an opportunity for both the employee and the company to assess role suitability and performance.

## Role Expectations

During probation, employees should become familiar with their responsibilities, team processes, tools, and workplace expectations.

Managers should provide reasonable guidance and clarify role expectations.

## Performance Review

The manager may review the employee's performance during the probation period.

The review may consider work quality, reliability, communication, teamwork, learning progress, and completion of assigned responsibilities.

## Feedback

Managers should provide feedback during the probation period and identify areas where improvement or additional support may be required.

Employees are encouraged to ask questions and discuss challenges with their manager.

## Completion of Probation

At the end of the probation period, the employee's performance and overall suitability may be reviewed.

The company may confirm the employee's continuation, extend the probation period where appropriate, or take other action according to applicable employment procedures.

## Extension

A probation period may be extended when additional time is reasonably required to assess performance or role suitability.

The employee should be informed about the reason and expected duration of the extension.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for meeting role expectations and actively participating in feedback and development discussions.

Managers are responsible for providing guidance, reviewing performance fairly, and communicating expectations clearly.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when employment procedures or organizational requirements change.
