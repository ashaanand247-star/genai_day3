# TechNova Solutions - Attendance Policy

Document ID: DOC003

## Purpose

The Attendance Policy defines the basic expectations for employee attendance, working hours, and communication regarding absences.

## Working Hours

Employees are expected to follow the working hours assigned to their team or role. Employees should begin work on time and remain available during their scheduled hours.

## Attendance Recording

Employees should record their attendance using the approved attendance system. Attendance records should accurately reflect the employee's working status.

## Late Arrival

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their manager or team as soon as possible. Repeated late arrivals may require a discussion with the manager.

## Unplanned Absence

If an employee cannot attend work because of an unexpected situation, the employee should notify their manager or the designated team contact as soon as reasonably possible.

## Planned Absence

Planned absences should be communicated in advance and, when applicable, submitted through the company's approved leave system.

## Remote Employees

Employees working remotely should remain available during their assigned working hours and follow the same attendance and communication expectations applicable to their team.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for maintaining accurate attendance records and communicating unexpected absences.

Managers are responsible for addressing attendance concerns consistently and discussing recurring issues with employees.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy based on operational requirements and changes to internal procedures.
