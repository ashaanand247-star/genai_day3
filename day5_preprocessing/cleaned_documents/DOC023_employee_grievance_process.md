# TechNova Solutions - Employee Grievance Process

Document ID: DOC023

## Purpose

The Employee Grievance Process provides a structured way for employees to raise workplace concerns and seek appropriate resolution.

## Raising a Concern

Employees who have a workplace concern should first consider whether it can be resolved through a discussion with their manager or another appropriate person.

For concerns that cannot be resolved informally, the employee may use the designated grievance process.

## Grievance Submission

A formal grievance should provide enough information to understand the concern, including relevant dates, circumstances, and supporting information where appropriate.

Employees should submit grievances through the approved company channel.

## Review

The designated reviewer may assess the grievance and determine the appropriate next steps.

Additional information may be requested from the employee or other relevant individuals.

## Confidentiality

Grievance information should be handled appropriately and shared only with individuals who need the information to review or resolve the concern.

Employees should avoid discussing sensitive grievance details with people who are not involved in the process.

## Investigation

Where necessary, the company may conduct an appropriate review or investigation.

Relevant individuals may be asked to provide information or participate in discussions.

## Resolution

After reviewing the available information, the company may communicate the outcome or next steps to the employee.

The resolution may include corrective actions, clarification, mediation, or other appropriate measures.

## Escalation

If an employee believes that a concern has not been appropriately addressed, they may use the applicable escalation process.

## Non-Retaliation

Employees should be able to raise genuine workplace concerns without fear of inappropriate retaliation.

Concerns about retaliation should be reported through the appropriate company channel.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for providing accurate information and participating appropriately in the grievance process.

Managers and designated reviewers are responsible for handling concerns fairly and according to applicable procedures.

## Process Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this process based on organizational requirements and feedback.
