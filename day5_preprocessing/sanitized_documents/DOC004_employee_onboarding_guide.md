# TechNova Solutions - Employee Onboarding Guide

Document ID: DOC004

## Purpose

The Employee Onboarding Guide describes the activities that help new employees understand their role, team, workplace procedures, and company resources.

## Before Joining

Before the employee's joining date, the relevant team should prepare required access, equipment, and onboarding information.

The employee may receive instructions about the joining process and documents that need to be completed.

## First Day

On the first day, the employee should complete the required joining formalities and meet the relevant team members.

The manager or assigned onboarding coordinator should explain the employee's role, responsibilities, working schedule, and immediate priorities.

## System Access

Employees may require access to approved systems such as email, collaboration tools, project systems, and internal applications.

Access should be provided according to the employee's role. Employees should not request or use access that is not required for their responsibilities.

## Team Introduction

The manager or onboarding coordinator should introduce the new employee to the team and explain the team's communication channels, regular meetings, and working practices.

## Training

New employees may be required to complete company training related to security, workplace practices, tools, and their specific role.

Employees should complete mandatory training within the assigned time period.

## First Week

During the first week, the employee should become familiar with their responsibilities, team processes, documentation, and required tools.

The manager should provide guidance and answer questions related to the employee's initial responsibilities.

## First Month

During the first month, the employee should gradually take ownership of assigned tasks and become familiar with the team's regular workflow.

The manager may conduct an informal check-in to discuss progress, questions, and areas where additional support is required.

## Responsibilities

The employee is responsible for completing assigned onboarding activities and asking questions when clarification is needed.

The manager is responsible for providing appropriate guidance, access, and role-related information.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may update the onboarding process when internal systems, procedures, or business requirements change.
