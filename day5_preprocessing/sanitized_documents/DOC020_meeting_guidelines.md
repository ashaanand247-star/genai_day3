# TechNova Solutions - Meeting Guidelines

Document ID: DOC020

## Purpose

The Meeting Guidelines describe practices for planning, conducting, and documenting effective team meetings.

## Meeting Planning

Meetings should have a clear purpose and include only the people whose participation is relevant to the discussion.

The organizer should provide the meeting topic and, when appropriate, an agenda before the meeting.

## Invitations

Meeting invitations should include the expected date, time, participants, and meeting purpose.

Participants should review the invitation and relevant materials before joining.

## Attendance

Participants should join scheduled meetings on time and remain engaged during the discussion.

If a participant cannot attend, they should inform the organizer when possible.

## Agenda

The organizer should use an agenda for meetings that require structured discussion.

The agenda may include topics, expected outcomes, and the approximate time allocated to each topic.

## Discussion

Participants should allow others an opportunity to contribute and should keep discussions focused on the meeting objectives.

Questions or topics that require a separate discussion may be moved to another meeting or handled through an appropriate communication channel.

## Decisions

Important decisions made during a meeting should be recorded so that relevant team members understand the outcome.

The person responsible for recording decisions should ensure that important actions are clearly documented.

## Action Items

Action items should identify the required task and, when appropriate, the person responsible and expected completion date.

Participants should communicate if an assigned action is delayed or requires additional support.

## Meeting Notes

For important meetings, notes should be stored in an approved team location.

Meeting notes should focus on relevant decisions, actions, and information rather than unnecessary details.

## Responsibilities

Meeting organizers are responsible for providing a clear purpose and coordinating the meeting.

Participants are responsible for preparing, participating appropriately, and completing assigned actions.

## Guideline Review

TechNova Solutions may review these guidelines when team collaboration practices or communication tools change.
