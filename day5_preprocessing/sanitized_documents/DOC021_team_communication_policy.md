# TechNova Solutions - Team Communication Policy

Document ID: DOC021

## Purpose

The Team Communication Policy provides guidelines for effective and professional communication between employees and teams.

## Approved Communication Channels

Employees should use approved company communication tools for work-related discussions.

The communication channel should be selected based on the type and urgency of the information.

## Email

Email may be used for formal communication, important announcements, approvals, and information that needs a clear written record.

Employees should use meaningful subject lines and include the relevant recipients.

## Team Messaging

Approved team messaging tools may be used for quick questions, coordination, and day-to-day discussions.

Important decisions should be documented in an appropriate team system when they need to be available for future reference.

## Meetings

Meetings should be used when real-time discussion or collaboration is useful.

Participants should follow the applicable meeting guidelines and contribute respectfully.

## Response Expectations

Employees should respond to work-related communication within a reasonable period based on the urgency and nature of the request.

Urgent matters should use the appropriate communication channel rather than relying only on routine messages.

## Professional Communication

Employees should communicate respectfully and professionally.

Messages should be clear, relevant, and appropriate for the intended audience.

## Confidential Information

Employees should not share confidential or restricted information through unauthorized communication channels.

Before sending sensitive information, employees should verify the recipients and the security of the communication method.

## Documentation

Important project decisions, requirements, and actions should be recorded in approved team documentation systems.

This helps team members access relevant information when needed.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for using communication tools appropriately and keeping relevant team members informed.

Managers are responsible for establishing clear communication practices within their teams.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when communication tools, team structures, or business practices change.
