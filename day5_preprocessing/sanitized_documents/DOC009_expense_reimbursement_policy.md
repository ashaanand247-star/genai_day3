# TechNova Solutions - Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: DOC009

## Purpose

The Expense Reimbursement Policy explains how employees can submit eligible business expenses for reimbursement.

## Eligible Expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable expenses incurred while performing approved business activities.

Examples may include approved travel, business meals, transportation, and other necessary work-related expenses.

## Prior Approval

Certain expenses may require approval before they are incurred.

Employees should confirm the applicable approval requirement with their manager when they are unsure whether an expense is eligible.

## Receipts

Employees should retain receipts or other acceptable supporting documents for expenses submitted for reimbursement.

Expense claims should contain accurate information about the date, amount, purpose, and business reason for the expense.

## Submission Process

Employees should submit expense claims through the approved company expense system.

Claims should be submitted within the applicable submission period after the expense is incurred.

## Review and Approval

The employee's manager or designated reviewer may review the claim to confirm that the expense is business-related and complies with company guidelines.

Finance or another authorized team may conduct additional checks before reimbursement is processed.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal expenses, unsupported expenses, and expenses that do not comply with company requirements may not be reimbursed.

Employees should not submit duplicate claims for the same expense.

## Corrections

If an employee identifies an error in a submitted expense claim, they should contact the appropriate support team and provide the corrected information.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for submitting accurate and complete expense claims.

Managers and reviewers are responsible for reviewing claims consistently and according to applicable company procedures.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when financial procedures or business requirements change.
