# TechNova Solutions - Workplace Health and Safety

Document ID: DOC022

## Purpose

The Workplace Health and Safety document provides general guidelines for maintaining a safe and healthy working environment at TechNova Solutions.

## Safe Workplace

Employees should keep their work areas reasonably clean and free from hazards.

Equipment, furniture, and other workplace resources should be used according to applicable instructions.

## Emergency Procedures

Employees should familiarize themselves with emergency exits, assembly areas, and relevant emergency procedures for their workplace.

During an emergency, employees should follow instructions from authorized safety or facilities personnel.

## Reporting Hazards

Employees should report unsafe conditions, damaged equipment, spills, blocked exits, or other workplace hazards to the appropriate facilities or safety contact.

Reports should be made as soon as reasonably possible.

## First Aid

Employees who require first aid should contact the designated workplace support or emergency service according to the applicable site procedure.

Emergency contact information should be available through approved workplace resources.

## Ergonomics

Employees should arrange their workstation to support comfortable and safe working practices.

Employees who experience difficulties with their workstation setup should discuss the issue with the appropriate manager or facilities team.

## Remote Work Safety

Employees working remotely should maintain a reasonably safe and suitable workspace.

Electrical equipment and company-provided devices should be used according to applicable safety instructions.

## Incident Reporting

Workplace incidents should be reported through the appropriate company process.

Employees should provide accurate information about the incident and cooperate with reasonable follow-up activities.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for following workplace safety procedures and reporting hazards.

Managers and facilities teams are responsible for supporting safe working conditions and responding to reported concerns.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update these guidelines when workplace conditions, safety requirements, or company procedures change.
