# TechNova Solutions - Holiday Policy

Document ID: DOC007

## Purpose

The Holiday Policy explains how company holidays are communicated and how employees should plan their work around scheduled holidays.

## Holiday Calendar

TechNova Solutions publishes an annual holiday calendar for employees. The calendar identifies the holidays applicable to the relevant teams or locations.

Employees should refer to the latest approved holiday calendar when planning leave or work activities.

## Company Holidays

Employees are generally not expected to work on designated company holidays unless their role or business requirements require coverage.

Teams that require holiday coverage should plan schedules in advance.

## Regional Holidays

Employees working in different locations may have different holidays based on applicable regional requirements and company arrangements.

Employees should follow the holiday calendar applicable to their assigned location.

## Working During a Holiday

Some teams may require employees to work on a scheduled holiday because of customer support, operational requirements, or other business needs.

Managers should communicate such requirements in advance whenever possible.

## Leave Planning

Employees should consider company holidays when planning annual leave.

A holiday does not normally need to be submitted as an individual leave request when it is already included in the applicable company holiday calendar.

## Communication

Changes to the holiday calendar will be communicated through approved company communication channels.

Employees should check official announcements for any changes.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for reviewing the applicable holiday calendar and planning their work and leave accordingly.

Managers are responsible for communicating team-specific coverage requirements.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may update the holiday calendar or related procedures when business or regional requirements change.
