# TechNova Solutions - Customer Data Handling

Document ID: DOC029

## Purpose

The Customer Data Handling Guidelines describe how employees should handle customer information during their work.

## Access

Employees should access customer information only when it is required for their assigned responsibilities.

Access should be limited to approved systems and authorized users.

## Data Collection

Employees should collect only the customer information required for a legitimate business purpose.

Information should be entered accurately into approved company systems.

## Data Storage

Customer information should be stored only in approved company systems and locations.

Employees should not copy customer information to unauthorized personal devices, applications, or storage services.

## Data Sharing

Customer information should only be shared with authorized employees, teams, or approved third parties when there is a legitimate business requirement.

Before sharing information, employees should verify the recipient and the approved communication method.

## Customer Requests

Customer requests involving their information should be handled through the appropriate company process.

Employees should not make commitments about data requests unless they are authorized to do so.

## Security

Employees should follow applicable security requirements when accessing or handling customer information.

Suspicious access, accidental disclosure, or other security concerns should be reported immediately through the designated security channel.

## Data Retention

Customer information should be retained according to applicable company requirements and approved business processes.

Information that is no longer required should be handled according to the applicable retention and disposal procedures.

## Remote Work

Employees working remotely must ensure that customer information cannot be viewed or accessed by unauthorized individuals.

Customer information should only be accessed through approved systems and secure access methods.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected loss, unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or incorrect transmission of customer information should be reported as soon as possible.

Employees should provide accurate information to help the appropriate team assess the incident.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for handling customer information carefully and following applicable company procedures.

Managers and authorized data owners are responsible for ensuring that appropriate data handling practices are followed within their teams.

## Guideline Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update these guidelines when customer requirements, security practices, or data handling procedures change.
