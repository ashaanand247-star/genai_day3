# TechNova Solutions - Data Protection Policy

Document ID: DOC017

## Purpose

The Data Protection Policy provides guidelines for handling company and customer information responsibly and securely.

## Data Classification

Information should be handled according to its sensitivity and business purpose.

Employees should understand whether information is intended for public, internal, confidential, or otherwise restricted use before sharing it.

## Access

Employees should access information only when it is required for their assigned responsibilities.

Access permissions should be appropriate to the employee's role.

## Data Sharing

Company and customer information should only be shared with authorized individuals or approved systems.

Employees should verify recipients before sending sensitive information.

## Storage

Business information should be stored using approved company systems and locations.

Employees should avoid storing sensitive company information on unauthorized personal devices or services.

## Data Transmission

When sensitive information needs to be transmitted, employees should use approved secure communication methods.

Employees should not send restricted information through unauthorized channels.

## Data Retention

Information should be retained according to applicable company requirements and business needs.

When information is no longer required, it should be handled according to the appropriate disposal procedure.

## Security Incidents

Suspected loss, unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or other data security incidents should be reported through the designated security or IT channel as soon as possible.

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for handling information according to company requirements and reporting suspected data protection concerns.

## Management Responsibilities

Managers should ensure that employees understand the data handling requirements relevant to their roles.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when business requirements, technology, or data protection practices change.
