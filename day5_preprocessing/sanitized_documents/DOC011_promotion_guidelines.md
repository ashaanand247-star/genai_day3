# TechNova Solutions - Promotion Guidelines

Document ID: DOC011

## Purpose

The Promotion Guidelines describe the general factors considered when evaluating employees for increased responsibilities or progression to another role.

## Performance

Consistent performance in the employee's current role is an important consideration.

Managers may review the quality, reliability, and impact of the employee's work over the relevant review period.

## Skills and Knowledge

Employees may be considered for progression when they demonstrate the skills and knowledge required for the responsibilities of the proposed role.

Relevant technical, professional, communication, and collaboration skills may be considered.

## Responsibilities

Employees who regularly take ownership of responsibilities beyond their current role may be considered for additional responsibilities or progression.

Taking on additional work does not automatically result in a promotion.

## Business Requirements

Promotion decisions may also depend on organizational needs, available roles, team structure, and business requirements.

## Review Process

Managers may discuss potential progression during performance reviews or other appropriate career discussions.

The employee's performance history, skills, responsibilities, and readiness may be reviewed as part of the process.

## Development

When an employee is not yet ready for progression, the manager may identify specific skills or experiences that can help the employee prepare for future opportunities.

## Fairness

Promotion decisions should be based on relevant performance and role requirements and should be handled consistently.

## Communication

Managers should communicate career development discussions and decisions clearly to employees.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for discussing career goals with their manager and working on agreed development areas.

Managers are responsible for providing appropriate feedback and guidance.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review these guidelines when organizational structures, roles, or career development processes change.
