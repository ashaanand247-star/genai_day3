# TechNova Solutions - Office Access Guidelines

Document ID: DOC018

## Purpose

The Office Access Guidelines describe the basic requirements for entering and using TechNova Solutions office facilities.

## Access Cards

Employees who are issued an office access card should use it only for authorized access.

Employees should not lend their access card to another person.

## Visitors

Visitors should follow the company's visitor procedures and may need to be accompanied by an authorized employee.

Employees should not allow unknown individuals to enter restricted areas without appropriate authorization.

## Restricted Areas

Some areas of the office may be restricted to authorized employees or specific teams.

Employees should only enter restricted areas when their responsibilities require access.

## Lost Access Cards

Employees should report a lost or stolen access card to the appropriate facilities or security team as soon as possible.

The access card may be disabled to prevent unauthorized use.

## After-Hours Access

Employees who need to access the office outside normal working hours should follow the applicable after-hours access procedure.

Additional authorization may be required depending on the location and business requirement.

## Security

Employees should not bypass physical security controls or allow unauthorized people to use their access credentials.

Suspicious activity around office entrances or restricted areas should be reported to the appropriate security contact.

## Workplace Conduct

Employees should follow applicable workplace safety and conduct requirements while using company facilities.

Shared spaces should be kept reasonably clean and accessible to other employees.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for protecting their access credentials and following office security procedures.

Facilities or security teams are responsible for managing access controls and responding to reported access issues.

## Guideline Review

TechNova Solutions may update these guidelines when office locations, security controls, or access procedures change.
