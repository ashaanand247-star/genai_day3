# TechNova Solutions - Recruitment and Referral Policy

Document ID: DOC024

## Purpose

The Recruitment and Referral Policy describes the general process for identifying, evaluating, and referring candidates for open positions at TechNova Solutions.

## Job Openings

Open positions should be communicated through approved recruitment channels.

Job descriptions should provide relevant information about the role, responsibilities, required skills, and applicable qualifications.

## Candidate Applications

Candidates should submit applications through the approved recruitment process.

Recruitment teams may review applications to determine whether candidates meet the basic requirements of the position.

## Interview Process

Shortlisted candidates may participate in one or more interviews or assessments.

The interview process may vary depending on the role and level of responsibility.

## Candidate Evaluation

Candidates should be evaluated against relevant role requirements.

Interviewers should provide objective feedback based on the candidate's skills, experience, and suitability for the position.

## Employee Referrals

Employees may refer qualified candidates for open positions through the approved referral process.

A referral does not guarantee an interview or selection.

Employees should provide accurate information about referred candidates and should not misrepresent a candidate's qualifications.

## Conflict of Interest

Employees involved in recruitment should disclose any personal relationship or other situation that could affect their ability to participate objectively in the hiring process.

## Candidate Information

Candidate information should be handled appropriately and accessed only by authorized individuals involved in recruitment.

Recruitment information should not be shared through unauthorized channels.

## Selection

The final hiring decision should consider the requirements of the role, candidate evaluation results, business needs, and applicable company procedures.

## Responsibilities

Recruitment teams are responsible for coordinating the hiring process.

Hiring managers are responsible for defining role requirements and participating in candidate evaluation.

Employees making referrals are responsible for providing accurate referral information.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when recruitment processes, organizational requirements, or applicable procedures change.
