# TechNova Solutions - Asset Management Policy

Document ID: DOC027

## Purpose

The Asset Management Policy explains how company-owned equipment and other assigned assets should be managed throughout their use.

## Asset Assignment

Company assets may be assigned to employees based on their role and business requirements.

The employee receiving an asset should verify that the asset information is recorded correctly.

## Asset Identification

Assets may be identified using an asset number, serial number, or another approved identification method.

Asset records should be maintained accurately so that equipment can be traced to the appropriate location or user.

## Responsible Use

Employees should use company assets responsibly and for authorized business purposes.

Employees should take reasonable precautions to prevent loss, damage, or unauthorized use.

## Maintenance

Employees should report equipment problems through the approved IT or facilities support process.

Employees should not make unauthorized modifications to company equipment.

## Asset Transfer

When an employee changes roles or equipment is reassigned, the asset record should be updated through the appropriate process.

Employees should return assets when instructed to support a transfer or reassignment.

## Lost or Damaged Assets

Employees should report lost, stolen, or significantly damaged company assets as soon as possible.

The appropriate team may review the incident and determine the required next steps.

## Asset Return

Company assets should be returned when they are no longer required or when an employee leaves the company.

The relevant team may verify the condition and return of the asset.

## Asset Records

Asset information should be maintained in the approved asset management system.

Records should be updated when assets are assigned, transferred, repaired, replaced, or returned.

## Responsibilities

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of assigned assets and reporting issues promptly.

IT, facilities, or other authorized teams are responsible for maintaining asset records and supporting asset management processes.

## Policy Review

TechNova Solutions may review and update this policy when equipment, asset management systems, or business requirements change.
